# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful experience for
everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the technical issue and kind about the person.
- Give review feedback that explains the reason and a possible fix.
- Assume good faith; ask a question before assuming a motive.
- Credit work and cite sources.
- Keep discussion in the issue or PR where the decision is recorded.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion after a maintainer asks you to stop.
- Sharing credentials, private repository data or client material.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block participation.
A report should go to the repository maintainer through a private channel; do
not open a public issue about a conduct report that contains personal details.
